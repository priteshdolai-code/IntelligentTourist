Explore Punjab: A Journey Through India's Jewel

Welcome to Punjab, the land of rich history, vibrant culture, and delectable cuisine that will tantalize your taste buds and leave you mesmerized with its warm hospitality and lively spirit.

Itinerary per person

  • Day 1: Arrival in Amritsar, visit the Golden Temple and witness the evening ceremony at the Wagah Border ₹3500
  • Day 2: Explore the Jallianwala Bagh memorial and Ram Bagh Gardens, visit the Partition Museum ₹2000
  • Day 3: Travel to Chandigarh, visit the Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, and Rose Garden ₹2500
  • Day 4: Explore the Punjab State War Heroes Memorial and Museum, visit the Chhatbir Zoo, and departure from Chandigarh ₹3000
