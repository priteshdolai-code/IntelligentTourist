Explore Odisha: A Journey Through India's Jewel

Welcome to Odisha, where ancient temples, pristine beaches, and rich cultural heritage await to captivate your senses and leave you with unforgettable memories.

Itinerary per person

  • Day 1: Arrival in Bhubaneswar, visit Lingaraj Temple and Mukteshwar Temple ₹3000
  • Day 2: Explore Udayagiri Caves, visit Nandankanan Zoological Park ₹2500
  • Day 3: Travel to Puri, visit Jagannath Temple and relax on Puri Beach ₹3500
  • Day 4: Visit Konark Sun Temple, relax in Konark Beach, and departure from Bhubaneswar ₹4000
