Explore Sikkim: A Journey Through India's Jewel

Welcome to Sikkim, a breathtaking Himalayan state nestled in the northeastern part of India. With its towering snow-capped peaks, lush green valleys, and vibrant Buddhist culture.

Itinerary per person

  • Day 1: Arrival in Gangtok, visit Rumtek Monastery and Namgyal Institute of Tibetology ₹3500
  • Day 2: Explore Tsomgo Lake and Baba Mandir, visit the Banjhakri Waterfall ₹4000
  • Day 3: Excursion to Nathula Pass (subject to permit availability), visit the Indo-China border ₹4500
  • Day 4: Travel to Pelling, visit Pemayangtse Monastery, and Rabdentse Ruins ₹3000
  • Day 5: Explore Khecheopalri Lake,Kanchenjunga Falls,Singshore Bridge ₹3500
  • Day 6: Visit the Buddha Park in Ravangla, explore Ravangla town, and departure from Gangtok ₹2500
